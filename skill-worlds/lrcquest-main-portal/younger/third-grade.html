🍎 Teacher Info — Third Grade

Strand: Reading & Literature — Fiction and nonfiction

Learning target: Distinguish fiction, informational texts, and reference resources.

I Can: I can distinguish fiction, informational texts, and reference resources.

Curriculum connection: I-SAIL 4 · AASL Include / Explore · ISTE 1.6

Assessment: Students answer six classification, feature, or comparison questions with immediate corrective feedback. Watch for which types of evidence students use in discussion.

Differentiation: Read prompts aloud for emerging readers; model one example together; allow partner discussion; extend by asking students to locate a real book in the LRC that matches the category.

This game uses original, fictional scenarios and does not require student accounts.
