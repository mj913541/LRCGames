👩‍🏫 Teacher & Standards

Grade: Kindergarten

Strand: Reading & Literature — Fiction and Nonfiction

Learning target / I Can: I can sort books that tell pretend stories from books that teach real information.

Curriculum connections: I-SAIL 4; AASL Include / Explore; ISTE 1.6 (creative communication). These are broad alignments, not claims that the game assesses every indicator.

Assessment: Observe student decisions, listen to explanations, and review points and corrective feedback. Scores are not saved.

Differentiation: Picture clues, listening support from a teacher, and two clear categories.

Digital citizenship: Consider audience, purpose, evidence, and safe sharing at an age-appropriate level.
