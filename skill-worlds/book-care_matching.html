📋 Teacher & Standards

🎓 Grade Level

Kindergarten–2nd Grade

📚 Strand

Information Literacy — Library Citizenship & Book Care

🎯 Skill

Identifying and recognizing behaviors that help keep library books clean, safe, and usable.

🌟 Learning Target

Students will identify and match illustrations showing appropriate book care practices.

💬 I Can Statement

"I can recognize ways to take care of library books so everyone can enjoy them."

📖 AASL Standards Connection

Shared Foundation III — Collaborate: Learners demonstrate respect for shared resources and their learning community.

Shared Foundation VI — Engage: Learners develop responsible practices for participating in a learning community and using shared resources.

These are instructional connections to the AASL Shared Foundations rather than direct quotations of individual indicators.

💻 ISTE Standards Connection

1.2 Digital Citizen: Students practice responsible behavior and respect for shared learning resources. Book care provides a concrete introduction to stewardship before applying similar responsibility to digital resources.

1.1 Empowered Learner: Students use an interactive learning activity to practice a skill and monitor progress through their matching results.

🔎 Assessment Evidence

  • Student correctly identifies all six matching pairs.
  • Student distinguishes between different book care practices.
  • Student completes the matching game independently or with appropriate support.
  • Teacher may ask students to explain why each practice protects books.

🌈 Differentiation

Additional Support:

  • Preview the six book care rules before beginning.
  • Use the audio directions and visual examples.
  • Allow partner play or teacher-guided matching.
  • Encourage students to describe pictures aloud before choosing.

On-Level:

  • Complete all six pairs independently.
  • Identify the book care behavior shown in each match.

Extension:

  • Explain what might happen if a book care rule is ignored.
  • Compare ways to care for printed books and Chromebooks.
  • Create an additional book care rule or illustration.
