🎯 Skill

Book Parts & Their Jobs

📖 Learning Target

Students will identify and explain the front cover, back cover, spine, title, author, and illustrator. Students also explore illustrations, publisher, copyright page, and table of contents as enrichment.

⭐ I Can Statement

I can name the parts of a book and explain what each part does!

📘 AASL Standards Connection

Shared Foundation: Inquire. Students build knowledge and vocabulary about how books are organized. This is a broad instructional connection, not a claim of a specific performance indicator.

💻 ISTE Standards Connection

1.1 Empowered Learner. Students use a digital learning activity to practice vocabulary and check their understanding. This is a broad instructional connection.

📝 Assessment Evidence

Successful matches show recognition of book-part images. Listening to spoken explanations builds understanding. Ask students to explain a part's job aloud to assess the full learning target.

🌈 Differentiation

Easy: 4 matching pairs (8 cards).
Medium: 7 matching pairs (14 cards).
Hard: 10 matching pairs (20 cards).
The selected book parts change each round.
