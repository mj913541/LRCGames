📋 Teacher & Standards

Grade

Kindergarten–First Grade

Strands

Technology Skills & Information Literacy

Skills

Mouse control, click-and-hold, drag-and-drop, visual matching, and recognizing parts of a book.

Learning Target

Students will use a mouse or trackpad to move puzzle pieces accurately while identifying familiar parts of a book.

⭐ I Can Statement

I can click, hold, and drag puzzle pieces to complete a picture of a book part!

AASL Connection

Inquire — building knowledge about books and their organization. Broad instructional connection.

ISTE Connection

1.1 Empowered Learner — practicing technology skills through an independent digital activity. Broad instructional connection.

Assessment

Observe accurate click-and-hold, dragging, releasing, and successful puzzle placement. Ask students to name the completed book part.

Differentiation

Level 1: 4 pieces (2 × 2).
Level 2: 6 pieces (3 × 2).
Level 3: 9 pieces (3 × 3).
No countdown timer.
