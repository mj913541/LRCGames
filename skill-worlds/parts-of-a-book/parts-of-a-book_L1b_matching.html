Grade & Strand

First Grade · Information Literacy

Skill

Book Parts & Their Jobs

Learning Target

Identify and explain front cover, back cover, spine, title, author, and illustrator. Illustrations, publisher, copyright page, and table of contents provide enrichment.

⭐ I Can

I can name the parts of a book and explain what each part does!

AASL Connection

Shared Foundation: Inquire — developing understanding of book organization and vocabulary (broad instructional alignment).

ISTE Connection

1.1 Empowered Learner — using a digital activity to practice and check understanding (broad instructional alignment).

Assessment

Matching a vocabulary card to a different job-description card demonstrates recognition of purpose. Ask students to explain a job in their own words to assess independent explanation.

Differentiation

Easy: 4 pairs; Medium: 7 pairs; Hard: 10 pairs. Randomized each round. Spoken text with listening lock.
