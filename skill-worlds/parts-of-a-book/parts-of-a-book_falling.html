📋 Teacher & Standards

Grade & Strand

Kindergarten–First Grade · Information Literacy & Technology Skills

Learning Target

Students will use mouse movement and single-click accuracy to identify book parts from pictures and vocabulary.

⭐ I Can

I can move my mouse and click the picture that matches a book-part word!

Skills

Mouse movement, aiming, single clicking, visual recognition, vocabulary, and hand-eye coordination.

AASL / ISTE Connections

AASL Inquire (book vocabulary); ISTE 1.1 Empowered Learner (independent digital skill practice). Broad instructional connections.

Differentiation & Assessment

Three progressively faster levels, five hearts, no scrolling, and spoken directions. Observe accurate clicks and students' recognition of book parts.
