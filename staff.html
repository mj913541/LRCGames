⬇️ Tech Prep & Troubleshooting

Start-of-Year Chromebook Tips:

  • Plug in your Chromebook 1–2 days before returning. If fully drained, it may need hours of charging before turning on.
  • Click the clock in the lower right to check for system updates.

Password Reset:

  • Visit mypassword.sd308.org to reset your password early.
  • Use your short username (e.g., malbrecht).
  • Password updates may take a few minutes across systems.
